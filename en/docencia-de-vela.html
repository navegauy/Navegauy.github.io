Training

Brevet (Uruguayan sailing licence)

Issued by the Prefectura Nacional Naval (Uruguayan coast guard). To practise the 100 exam questions: Brevet quiz (in Spanish).

Official requirements: ask the Prefectura Nacional Naval.

UTU: Nautical Sports Training

Area 2476T, register of teaching applicants for Montevideo, Maldonado, Rocha and Salto (applications closed on 17 Nov 2026).

Source: UTU Resolution 5590/2026 (in Spanish)

PET Arrayanes: Nautical Sports Technical Program

The Polo Educativo Tecnológico in Arrayanes (Maldonado) runs a technical program in nautical sports (Tecnicatura de Deportes Náuticos). Its students organize the Piriápolis Nautical Festival, free and open to the public, with a multiclass dinghy regatta.

Source: la diaria Maldonado (November 2026, in Spanish)

Free tools for schools

Entrenamiento a bordo (boat tuning, drills, starts, logbook), Táctica a bordo (racing tactics) and Revisión de seguridad (safety check). All in Spanish.
